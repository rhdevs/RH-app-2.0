# The New RHApp

### What's new — a tour of everything we've added to make hall life easier

The RHApp has grown up. What started as a simple room-booking tool is now a proper home for hall life — your CCAs, their events, your bookings, and your profile, all in one place. This page walks through everything that's new, in plain language. No jargon, just what you can now do.

---

## CCAs finally have a home

Every CCA now has its own space in the app — a real page it can call its own.

- **A proper CCA page.** Each CCA gets a banner, a logo, and a description that its heads write themselves. It looks the part, and it's the first thing people see.
- **Heads can make it their own.** CCA heads can upload a logo and a banner and edit their description right from the app — images are tidied up automatically so they always look sharp, even straight from a phone.
- **"My CCAs" for everyone.** Every resident gets a personal dashboard of the CCAs they're part of, shown as a clean grid of cards. If you also happen to lead one, it's marked with a badge and a shortcut to manage it.
- **Your CCAs on your profile.** The CCAs you belong to now show up on your profile, neatly grouped, with your role in each one.

## Running a CCA is easier

For the people who keep CCAs going, there's now a real toolkit instead of spreadsheets and group chats.

- **A head dashboard.** CCA heads get a dashboard for each CCA they run, with everything in one place — the roster, the description, applications, interviews, and events — and a quick switcher for heads who run more than one.
- **Manage your roster.** Heads can see exactly who's in their CCA and remove members — one at a time or several at once — with a clear confirmation so nothing happens by accident. The member count always matches the list you see, so the numbers you report are the real ones.
- **Hand over cleanly.** When it's time to pass the torch, a head can hand their CCA over to the next committee themselves. The app shows you exactly who's coming in and who's stepping out, and makes sure a CCA is never left with nobody in charge.
- **Honest about the messy bits.** If a membership record can't be matched to a person, the app shows it clearly rather than quietly dropping it — so nothing and nobody silently goes missing.

## Joining a CCA now happens in the app

The whole "apply, interview, get in" journey used to live in forms and DMs. It's now built right into the RHApp.

- **Browse and apply.** Residents can browse CCAs and apply straight from a CCA's page, with a spot to tell the committee a little about why they're interested. The app keeps you from accidentally applying twice or applying to something you're already in.
- **Book your own interview slot.** Once you've applied, you pick an interview time from the slots the CCA has opened — and reschedule or cancel it yourself if plans change.
- **Track where you stand.** A "My applications" page shows every CCA you've applied to and exactly where each one is — waiting for an interview, being reviewed, or done — so you're never left wondering.
- **Heads run the whole thing.** CCA heads open interview slots in bulk (pick a day, a time range, and how long each interview is, and the app lays them out), see their day as a clean schedule, review every application, take notes during interviews, and accept people straight into the CCA with a click. Accepted members are added to the roster automatically.

## Events: discover them, sign up, show up

A brand-new events system connects CCAs, the JCRC, and residents.

- **CCAs propose events.** A CCA head fills in the details — what, when, where, how many people — attaches a proposal, and sends it off for approval.
- **The JCRC reviews and approves.** Proposed events go to the JCRC, who approve or send them back with feedback, keeping the hall's calendar sensible and coordinated.
- **Rooms book themselves.** If an event is happening in a hall facility, approving it automatically reserves the room — no separate booking step, no double-bookings, no forgotten reservations.
- **A beautiful events timeline.** Residents get a scrolling timeline of what's coming up, with banners, dates, locations, and how many people are going. Filter it by upcoming events, the ones you're going to, or past ones.
- **One-tap sign-up.** Sign up (or change your mind) in a tap. Events with a capacity fill up fairly, and you'll never accidentally sign up twice.
- **Photos and details.** Each event has its own page with a banner, a full description, and a photo gallery.

## Bookings, improved

The original room-booking experience got some long-overdue love.

- **You can finally see your upcoming bookings.** Previously "Your Bookings" only showed past ones — you could book a room and then never see it again. Now you see everything, each clearly marked **Incoming** or **Completed**.
- **Find bookings faster.** Search by facility or event, filter by room, and choose how far back to look.
- **The right rooms for the right people.** Rooms that are meant for specific groups now correctly show up as available to those groups — cleaner and less confusing for everyone.

## Your profile

- **Edit your own details.** Update your display name, Telegram handle, bio, block, and matriculation number yourself — no need to ask anyone.
- **See your roles and CCAs at a glance.** Your profile clearly shows what you are in the hall (resident, CCA head, and so on) and every CCA you're part of.
- **Your matriculation number stays private.** It's shown hidden by default, with a tap to reveal — it's your identity credential, not a display field.

## Signing in

- **A smoother front door.** Signing in is tidier, and the app guides new accounts through a short, friendly setup so everyone starts off with the right details.
- **Clear messaging.** If an account isn't eligible for the hall app, it now sees a clear, polite explanation instead of a page that half-works.

## Quietly, it also got safer and more reliable

Not everything new is something you click — a lot of this month went into making the app trustworthy behind the scenes.

- **Your bookings are yours.** We closed a gap where booking details could be seen by the wrong person. Only you (and hall admins) can see your booking's details now.
- **One account per person.** We cleaned up years of accidental duplicate accounts, carefully merging everyone's bookings, gym visits, and CCA memberships onto a single account — without losing a thing.
- **Fewer silent glitches.** A whole category of quiet, hard-to-spot bugs was tracked down and fixed, and new safeguards were added so they can't creep back in. The result is an app that behaves the way you'd expect, consistently.

## What's new for you

The same app does different things depending on who you are in the hall. Here's what each group gets.

### For the JCRC

The JCRC now has a real command centre for keeping hall life running smoothly.

- **Approve events.** Every event a CCA proposes comes to the JCRC to approve or send back with feedback — so the hall calendar stays coordinated and nothing clashes.
- **Rooms sorted automatically.** When you approve an event that's using a hall facility, the room is booked for it there and then. No chasing, no separate step, no double-bookings.
- **See any CCA's roster.** Look up any CCA in the hall and see its heads and members in one place.
- **Manage who leads each CCA.** Appoint and remove CCA heads, including onboarding a whole new committee in one go by pasting in their names.
- **A clear record of everything.** Every important action is logged, so there's always an honest answer to "who changed what, and when."

### For CCA heads

CCA heads get the biggest upgrade — a proper toolkit for running a club instead of juggling chats and spreadsheets.

- **Your own CCA dashboard.** One place for everything about your CCA, with a quick switcher if you run more than one.
- **Make your CCA look great.** Upload a logo and banner and write your description yourself — images are cleaned up automatically so they always look sharp.
- **Run recruitment end to end.** Open interview slots in bulk, see your interview day as a clean schedule, review every application, take notes during interviews, and accept people straight into the CCA.
- **Manage your roster.** See exactly who's in your CCA and remove members individually or in bulk, with a confirmation so nothing happens by accident.
- **Put on events.** Propose an event, add its details and a proposal, and — once the JCRC approves — publish it with a banner, photos, and a description for residents to see.
- **Know your turnout.** For each event, see how many people signed up, how that grew over time, and which blocks they're from, and download the attendee list.
- **Hand over cleanly.** When your term ends, pass your CCA to the next committee yourself, with the app making sure it's never left leaderless.

### For residents

Everyone in the hall gets a friendlier, more useful app.

- **Discover and join CCAs.** Browse every CCA, apply in-app, book your own interview slot, and track exactly where your application stands.
- **See the CCAs you're in.** A personal "My CCAs" dashboard, plus your memberships shown on your profile.
- **Find and attend events.** Scroll a timeline of what's coming up, filter to the events you care about, and sign up (or change your mind) in a tap.
- **Book and manage rooms.** See your upcoming bookings — not just past ones — clearly marked, and search or filter to find what you need fast.
- **Own your profile.** Edit your name, Telegram, bio, block, and matriculation number yourself, with your matric number kept private.
- **A smoother start.** Signing in and setting up your account is quicker and clearer.
